# Q1 early revenue options

Date: 2026-10-03. Status: preparation and comparison only; no payment channel,
offer, contract, hosted network or token sale is activated by this document.

**Prioritize a small paid technical-support engagement and development
sponsorship with no token entitlement.** A bounded local evaluation service
can follow. These fund work/expertise without making Q1 units a product.
Whether anyone will pay remains unvalidated; open source alone is not demand.

## Assumptions and boundaries

The current deliverable is experimental LOCALNET software, with no independent
complete security audit, production custody or public networking. The user has
not yet supplied a contracting jurisdiction, business/individual structure or
eligible payout route for this comparison. Do not infer them from language,
timezone, account settings or code availability. No dedicated project email or
commercial intake is assumed to exist.

Timelines below are **planning estimates**, not forecasts or promises. They
start only after an eligible payer, contracting/tax/payment setup and relevant
technical prerequisites exist. Costs are incremental categories, not provider
quotes; they exclude the maintainer's labor and unknown legal/accounting costs.
Low setup cost does not mean no obligations. No donation is represented as
charitable or tax-deductible, and none buys future tokens, equity or returns.

## Payment/platform reality check

GitHub Sponsors is only a **conditional example**, not a confirmed route for
this maintainer. Its [contributor documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors-for-open-source-contributors)
limits receiving sponsorship to supported regions; its
[additional terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms)
require recipient information and impose eligibility/payment restrictions.
Verify the actual residence, recipient, account and payout route before choosing
it. No Sponsors enrollment, bank account, fiscal host or payment provider has
been verified for Q1. Do not use false residence, nominee accounts or another
route to evade eligibility or sanctions controls.

The legal review must be activity- and jurisdiction-specific.
[FATF guidance](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/Fatfrecommendations/Guidance-rba-virtual-assets-2021.html)
addresses virtual-asset activities and service-provider risks; it does not
decide a Q1 service's local obligations. In the EU,
[ESMA's MiCA materials](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica)
illustrate why offering/disclosure and service-provider questions need separate
assessment. Neither source establishes which jurisdiction applies to this project
or that any exemption/authorization is available.

## Smallest reviewable service candidate

Proposed deliverable: **Q1 LOCALNET reproduction and troubleshooting session**.
This is a draft scope, not a live offer or a sales message.

- Input: approved local/isolated test environment, synthetic data and a specific
  build, vector or recovery problem. Client retains control of its system/keys.
- Work: reproduce the pinned release, diagnose one bounded problem, run applicable
  tests and record commands, results, limitations and remaining issues.
- Output: a technical report and optional reviewable patch, with a written time
  cap and acceptance criteria. No security-audit certificate, custody, production
  SLA, token entitlement or promise of future value.
- Before accepting money: choose the contracting recipient/jurisdiction and an
  eligible payout route; agree scope, confidentiality, price/currency, invoicing,
  cancellation/refund and support terms. No price is selected by this document.
- Demand check: with separate authorization, seek one bounded pilot; measure
  willingness to pay, support time, actual costs and repeatability before buying
  infrastructure. No outreach or messages were sent in this phase.

Sponsorship can complement this work if eligibility is confirmed, with public
milestones and no ownership/return/token promises. Enterprise discovery can
follow demonstrated demand. Hosted networks, treasury and monetary distribution
should not be used to finance around unresolved operational/legal decisions.
