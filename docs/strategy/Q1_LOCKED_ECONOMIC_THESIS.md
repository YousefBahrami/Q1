# Q1 locked economic thesis — human decision

Recorded 2026-10-05. Approved strategic constraints; implementation is not implied.
The human-provided decision text is preserved verbatim below.

==================================================
LOCKED Q1 STRATEGIC DECISIONS — MUST BE PRESERVED
==================================================

The following decisions are already approved and must constrain all further
mining, testnet, economics and public-network work.

Do not reopen them unless a direct contradiction with security or executable
evidence is discovered.

1. Q1 IS NOT ONLY A COIN

Q1 must evolve toward three complementary economic roles:

A. settlement/payment for resources and services
B. service commitment assurance through bond/escrow mechanisms
C. coordination and settlement between machines and AI agents

Do not design mining/economics as an isolated token-emission system.

Mining/resource participation must eventually support the broader Q1
resource/service economy.

==================================================
2. RESOURCE MARKET AND SERVICE MARKET
==================================================

The intended Q1 economy includes two complementary layers:

RESOURCE MARKET
- storage
- CPU
- GPU
- bandwidth
- other measurable resources

SERVICE MARKET
- services/products built using those resources

Mining/resource design should be evaluated partly by whether it helps
bootstrap or strengthen these markets.

Do not collapse mining rewards and service/provider rewards into one concept.

==================================================
3. CONSENSUS MUST REMAIN DETERMINISTIC
==================================================

AI may later assist with:

- resource discovery
- matching
- scheduling
- price/reliability comparison
- distributed job orchestration
- splitting work between providers

But AI must NOT determine consensus validity.

Consensus-critical behavior remains deterministic and independently verifiable.

==================================================
4. RESOURCE CONTRIBUTION MUST NOT EQUAL POLITICAL POWER
==================================================

A participant contributing more storage/compute/resources may earn more
economic reward where appropriate.

But greater resource ownership must NOT automatically grant proportionally
greater consensus/governance authority.

Do not design a direct equation:

MORE RESOURCE = MORE CONSENSUS POWER

unless separately approved after explicit security analysis.

This constraint is particularly important in the Sybil/resource-identity work.

==================================================
5. INDIVIDUAL INCENTIVE MUST STRENGTHEN Q1
==================================================

The system should preferably create this flywheel:

participant seeks personal economic benefit
→ contributes useful resource/service
→ Q1 network capacity/reliability increases
→ more real activity becomes possible
→ demand for Q1 settlement/services increases

Avoid reward structures that encourage:

- useless hardware expenditure
- artificial identity splitting
- idle resource claims with no network value
- centralization for purely financial extraction

==================================================
6. NETWORK REVENUE SHOULD COME FROM REAL ACTIVITY
==================================================

Long-term ecosystem revenue should come primarily from useful activity such as:

- resource transactions
- service transactions
- small market/protocol fees
- node hosting
- monitoring
- deployment
- support
- development services
- sponsorship
- partnerships

Do not make Q1 economically dependent only on appreciation of Q1 Coin.

Do not intentionally make network usage expensive merely to increase revenue.

==================================================
7. MAINNET GATES
==================================================

Before Mainnet/value-bearing launch, the following must be resolved or tested:

- issuance
- mining/reward distribution
- participant/resource selection
- consensus/fault model
- treasury mechanics
- service/resource-market relationship
- legal distribution path

Do not let public-presence pressure bypass these gates.

==================================================
8. PRODUCT PRIORITY RULE
==================================================

Every significant new feature should materially contribute to at least one of:

- attracting/running network participants
- creating real demand for Q1
- creating sustainable revenue

If not, defer it unless required for correctness or security.

==================================================
9. CURRENT PATH REMAINS
==================================================

The strategic path remains:

Technology
→ Community
→ Public Testnet
→ Miners / Resource Participants
→ Scarcity / Useful Resource Contribution
→ Mainnet
→ Market

Do not jump directly from LOCALNET or research prototype to speculative sale.

==================================================
10. APPLY THESE TO THE CURRENT V1 WORK
==================================================

When evaluating v1 resource proof and Sybil resistance, also report:

- does the proposed resource contribution create useful capacity for Q1?
- is the resource potentially reusable in the future Resource Market?
- does the mechanism reward real network utility or only artificial scarcity?
- can the participant earn economically without gaining disproportionate
  consensus authority?
- could the same resource later support service-provider commitments/bonds?

Add a final section to the next report:

ALIGNMENT WITH LOCKED Q1 ECONOMIC THESIS

Do not rewrite the approved thesis.
Evaluate whether the new mining/resource design is consistent with it.
